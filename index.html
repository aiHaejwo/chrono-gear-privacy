iOS 기기에서는 Apple의 앱 추적 투명성(App Tracking Transparency, ATT) 권한 창을 통해 맞춤형 광고 허용 여부를 직접 선택하실 수 있으며, 권한을 거부하더라도 모든 게임 콘텐츠는 아무런 제한 없이 이용 가능합니다. 자세한 내용은 Google 개인정보처리방침을 참고해 주시기 바랍니다.

[English] Privacy Policy

1. No Account Required & Personal Data Policy

Chrono Gear: Astrolabe Bastion (Bundle ID: com.horologyatelier.chronogeardefense) requires no account registration or sign-in and does NOT collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library content to any developer server.

2. Optional Rewarded Ads (Google AdMob) & iOS ATT

This app integrates the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK solely to serve optional player-initiated Rewarded Video Ads. Google may process device advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS / AAID on Android), coarse IP-based location, product interaction, and diagnostic data for ad delivery, frequency capping, analytics, and fraud prevention.

On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt lets you choose whether to allow personalized ads; declining tracking never restricts any gameplay feature. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Local Storage & Instant Data Deletion

All gameplay progress and preferences are stored strictly locally inside your device's app sandbox with a SHA-256 integrity signature. You may permanently wipe all local save data at any time via [Settings → Reset All Local Data] inside the app or by uninstalling the app.

4. Fair Gameplay & Disclosed Probabilities

All in-run Gear Summons and Lucky Draws use earned gameplay currencies (Gold & Lucky Coils) exclusively, with zero real-money wagering or cash-out mechanics. Exact star probabilities are disclosed to two decimal places in [Summon Odds %].
